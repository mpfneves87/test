Our services

  1. Tax services

    Individual and corporate tax consulting and compliance is the cornerstone of our service offering — returns, provisional tax, disputes and SARS correspondence, handled properly and on time.

    • Individual and corporate income tax returns
    • Provisional tax calculations and submissions
    • Tax clearance and compliance status
    • SARS objections, disputes and correspondence

  2. Bookkeeping

    We prepare from various source documents — such as customer invoices, supplier invoices and bank statements — a full set of management accounts on a regular monthly basis. Your source documents are captured in Sage Pastel Partner. We strive to provide affordable services to our clients.

    • Monthly management accounts
    • Processing in Sage Pastel Partner
    • VAT and statutory submissions

  3. Wills, trusts & estates

    Getting the structure right long before it is tested, so that what you have built passes on the way you intended — without avoidable tax or delay.

    • Wills and testaments
    • Estate planning and administration
    • Registration and administration of trusts
    • Contracts and legal agreements
    • General legal consultation

  4. Expatriate tax

    Emigration, the foreign employment income exemption, ceasing tax residency and double-taxation relief. The rules move frequently and the cost of misreading them is high. We manage South African tax affairs for clients earning abroad.

How we work
