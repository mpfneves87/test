Accounting and payroll work is only as reliable as the systems beneath it.

Systems and support

Elkru Computer Services maintains the technology the rest of the group runs on, and the same team is available to the group's clients. The approach is the one you would expect of an accounting practice: keep it working, keep it backed up, and explain it plainly.

Our services

  1. Systems & support

    Day-to-day support for small and medium businesses, covering the systems that halt work when they fail.

  2. Hardware & software supply

    Sourcing, setting up and licensing the equipment and software a small office actually needs.

  3. Accounting system setup

    Installing and configuring accounting packages — including the Sage Pastel Partner environment the group's bookkeeping runs on — so your records and your accountant's agree.

Why it sits in the group

  • It already knows the accounting systems. They are the ones the group works in every day.
  • One call. Where a bookkeeping problem proves to be a systems problem, the client does not coordinate two suppliers.
